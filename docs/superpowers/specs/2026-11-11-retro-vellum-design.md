# Retro redesign: Vellum structure, 16-bit cast

Date: 2026-11-11. Status: approved in chat, pending review of this written spec.

## Intent

Tony wants the portfolio to feel like vellum.ai (calm editorial type, one action, a cast of characters that carries the brand, a short scroll story) but in his own 16-bit RPG world, using the pixel characters and isometric field the site already has. Recruiters must still scan the work in about 30 seconds. Success means the first screen is memorable and specific to him, the evidence features stay intact, and nothing reads as a template or as a copy of Vellum.

What Tony decided:
- **Theme:** both. Light is the default and dark stays as a toggle.
- **Retro flavour:** 16-bit RPG.
- **Game takeover:** an editorial page with an RPG cast, not a map visitors must play through.
- **Remaining choices:** use my recommendations ("recommend all").

## Facts the design rests on (checked)

- **Sprites:** the 8 characters exist as 64×80 crisp-edged SVGs (`images/portfolio-field/<role>.svg`). PNG idle sheets have 4 frames and walk sheets 6. All of them scale cleanly at integer multiples.
- **Art builder:** `scripts/build-portfolio-field-art.py` uses only the standard library. New sprites, frames and scenes are generated the same way, as original art, with `ASSETS.md` updated to match.
- **Fonts:** Pixelify Sans is on Google Fonts (`css2?family=Pixelify+Sans:wght@400..700` returns 200).
- **Dark mode:** the site defaults to dark today. The `<body>` starts with `dark-mode`, an inline script removes it when `localStorage.darkMode === 'disabled'`, and `js/script.js:37-52` handles the toggle.
- **Night palette:** the field already has night palettes (`field-summer-night.svg`, `field-snow-night.svg`).
- **Content:** there are 11 project cards (`.filterItem`). The lab log has 22 entries. The homepage visible-word budget is 1744, enforced in `check-portfolio-field.mjs`.

## Design

### 1. Theme: day by default, night on toggle

- Light ("day") is the default. A visitor who previously chose dark keeps it: `localStorage.darkMode === 'enabled'` adds `dark-mode` before first paint.
- Day palette: a warm paper ground, near-black ink, and one accent from the field (the field's green or terracotta, not purple or indigo). Night palette: the field's night colours.
- Every colour is a token on `:root` / `body.dark-mode`, per Hallmark rule 1.
- Contrast is 4.5:1 or better for body text and 3:1 for large text, in both modes.

### 2. Type: three faces, fixed roles

| Face | Role | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Pixelify Sans | Wordmark, section titles, RPG UI labels (menu tabs, dialogue name tags) | Sizes in whole-pixel steps (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96px). Never below 16px. Never body text. |
| Lato | Body copy, navigation, buttons | ≥16px body |
| Inconsolata | Dates, versions, counts | Data only |

Karla is retired. Press Start 2P is not used.

### 3. Hero: Vellum's shape, Tony's cast

- **Left column:**
  - "Tony Koo" in Pixelify Sans, 96px on desktop and 48px on phones
  - one line, "builds decision systems.", in Lato
  - the subtitle and positioning sentence as they are now, within the word budget
  - one primary button ("View projects") and one text link ("Discuss a project")
  - the proof line ("22 pages logged, latest added …")
- **Right and lower edge:** the isometric field widens to bleed past the hero's bottom edge, the way Vellum's swarm does. The 8 characters render at 2× (desktop) or 3× (wide screens) with nearest-neighbour scaling, and walk as they do now.
- **Kept:** dragging, the cast choice, seasons, keyboard focus, the dated status card and every existing `data-*` hook. `check-portfolio-field` and `check-isometric-field` must still pass. Their assertions may change only where a section of this spec says so.

### 4. RPG skins on existing content

No new claims or copy. The word budget still holds.

- **Pixel frame:** one reusable 9-slice border, generated by the art builder as an SVG used through `border-image`, with a 2px hard shadow. It is used for item cards, dialogue boxes and the quest log. It has day and night variants.
- **Projects become item cards:**
  - Each `.filterItem` keeps its evidence label inside the pixel frame. The card title is set in Pixelify Sans.
  - The filter chips become a game menu: the same buttons, styled as a menu row, with a pixel ► cursor before the active item. The cursor is CSS-only, with `aria-pressed`/`active` unchanged.
- **The lab log becomes a quest log:** the same entries and dates, each with a small pixel "completed" mark (an SVG icon, never an emoji). `check-evidence` keeps validating every date.
- **The field selection card becomes a dialogue box:** the pixel frame, a name tag in Pixelify Sans, and the character's still sprite as a portrait at 2×. Its text is unchanged ("Latest: …, added …").
- **Buttons press like console buttons:**
  - At rest, a 2px hard bottom shadow.
  - Pressed, `translateY(2px)` with no shadow (it settles into its shadow). This replaces the 0.97 scale.
  - Hover is a colour step only, with no lift.
  - Under reduced motion, the change is instant.

### 5. Two scroll chapters

- **Placement:** one before Projects (the AI Engineer) and one before Contact (the Student). There are only two.
- **Behaviour:** a single character at 8× (512×640 rendered from its SVG, `image-rendering: pixelated`) walks in from the side as the chapter scrolls through the viewport. A dialogue box shows that section's existing intro sentence.
- **Implementation:**
  - CSS scroll-driven animation (`animation-timeline: view()`) inside `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`.
  - Without support, or under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the character stands in place and the dialogue box is visible. The content never stays hidden.
  - No JS scroll listeners and no extra library.
- **Height:** a chapter is one screen tall at most. These are not Vellum's long empty stretches.

### 6. Kept out on purpose

- Vellum's googly-eyed blobs, its exact layout, Instrument Serif, and the cream-plus-serif look.
- Press Start 2P, CRT scanlines, typewriter text, confetti or particles, emoji icons.
- Any mechanic a visitor must play through to reach the work.
- A third scroll chapter, scroll-linked card reveals, glass panels, and purple or indigo accents.

**PR 3:**

## Risks

- **Pixel type is less readable.** Mitigation: never below 16px, and only for titles and labels.
- **Scroll-driven animation support.** Support varies by browser; current Chromium/Safari/Firefox status is unverified as of 2026-11-11. Mitigation: the static fallback is the designed state, not a degraded one, and the PR 3 checks run in Chromium only.
- **Field checks are fragile when the hero layout changes.** Mitigation: PR 1 changes only the field's container scale and placement, not its internal markup.

## Amendments from PR 1 (2026-11-12)
- **Sprite scale:** 2× everywhere at 900px and wider. There is no 3× tier, because the field is capped at 1000px and 3× stations overlap.
- **Hero layout:** the field is stacked full width under the intro, with its toolbar overlaid in the map's empty sky. A side column was too narrow for characters to move apart at 2×. Full width also matches Vellum's cast under the headline.
- **Karla:** retired on the homepage only in PR 1. Research, report and chat pages still load Karla. Retiring it there belongs to a later PR.

## Amendments from PR 2 (2026-11-12)
- **Portrait size:** the dialogue-box portrait renders at 1× (64×80). At 2× it would take over a 270px card. It is still a whole-number scale.
- **Where the pixel frame goes:** item cards and the dialogue box get the frame. The filter menu and the quest log keep their plain rows, with the ► cursor and the quest marks carrying the game feel. Framing every block would make the page heavy.
- **Console press:** applies to filled controls only (hero button, filter menu, cast/season toggles, contact links). Text-style links press without a hard shadow, because on a transparent link the shadow reads as a second underline.
