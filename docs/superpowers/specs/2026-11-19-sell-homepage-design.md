# Sell Homepage Design

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Supersedes:** sections 3 to 5 of `2026-11-11-retro-vellum-design.md` (pixel headings, pixel card frames, scroll chapters) for the homepage. The field, the avatar, the themes and the motion tokens stay.

## Goal

The first screen convinces a hiring manager for AI or quant engineering roles. Clients get one "work with me" route. The page shows few things, in one visual system, with results first.

## Why

Evaluation on 2026-11-19 against korolev.uk, jakub.kr and lawsofux.com:

- The best numbers sit about 60% down a 15,106px page (22,802px on phones).
- Project cards lead with disclaimers, not outcomes, and all 11 are about 900px tall.
- Three visual systems share one page: pixel, Lato-bold soft cards, and report pills.
- Thumbnails are look-alike generated diagrams.

## Decisions

1. **Audience.** Hiring managers first. One client route: the existing "Discuss a Project" link.
2. **Retro is an accent.** Keep the isometric field, the sprites, the avatar and the field's own dialogue frame. Pixelify Sans stays on the wordmark (`.title-word`) and inside the field (its title and the dialogue name tag) only. Every other heading is Lato 700.
3. **Structure follows jakub.kr, cards follow lawsofux.com.** One content column, one card style, one heading scale.
4. **Hero.**
   - Line under the name: `I build AI and quant systems whose results you can check.`
   - `.heroSubtitle` and `.heroPositioning` are removed.
   - A proof strip sits directly under the actions, with the three items now in `#proof`, wording unchanged, including the `~90%` qualifier.
   - `#proof` is then deleted.
5. **Four featured projects**, in this order, each a large card:
   1. Agentic Healthcare Analytics Chatbot
   2. Self-RL & RAG Extraction Pipelines
   3. Fixed Income Analytics: Yield Curve Modeling
   4. Judicial Analytics: Legal NLP
6. **Featured card content.** Art, title, one outcome line, one context line, stack, one link. The outcome line reuses a sentence or number already published on the homepage or in that project's report. No new claims.
7. **Other seven projects** are a compact list: title, one line, link. No images.
8. **Evidence labels leave the homepage.** "Evidence", "Does not claim", "Full label" and "Report added" move off the cards. The report pages already carry receipts.
9. **Card art.** One flat-colour geometric SVG per featured project, 800×500, generated by a stdlib Python script. Each uses two colours from the theme plus paper. No text inside the art.
10. **Removed.** Both chapters, the deck view and its toggle, the filter buttons, `.pixelFrame` on project cards, pixel chips.
11. **Collapsed.** Reading and Certifications each become one closed `<details>` under Resume.
12. **Nav.** Projects, Writing, Research, Find, Resume, Contact. Chat leaves the nav; the Ask launcher covers it.
13. **Details pass** (from the jakub.kr article):
    - `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `text-wrap: pretty` on paragraphs.
    - `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on numbers.
    - Nested radii are concentric: inner radius = outer radius − padding.
    - One border colour and one shadow for all cards.
14. **Budgets.**
    - Page height: at most 9,000px at 1440×900 and 13,000px at 390×844.
    - Visible words: at most 1,200.
    - The first proof number is inside the first 900px at 1440 and the first 1,700px at 390.

## Out of scope

Report and research page templates (second plan, same system). New project content. Real screenshots: the four featured projects are sanitized and have none.
