# UI flow test findings (live site, 2026-11-19)

Four agents tested https://lmdlifers.github.io/TonyKoo/ . This file records confirmed defects.

## Links (27 pages, 827 links, 177 anchors)

No internal link, anchor or asset is broken.

1. **Dead external link:** `https://smoodee.sg/` (homepage "More projects", Smoodee row). The domain does not resolve.
2. **Dead cited source:** `https://github.com/github/spec-kit/blob/main/docs/reference/workflows.md` in `research/from-specification-to-verified-release.html`. The docs moved to `docs/workflows/`.
3. **Dead licence link:** `https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/architecture-center/blob/main/LICENSE` in `research/how-to-choose-a-system-boundary.html`. The repo returns 404.
4. **No favicon on 26 pages.** Only `index.html` declares one; the others request `/favicon.ico` and get 404.
5. **Header link sets differ by page type.** Reports: Projects, Writing, Research. Essays: Essays, Start, Portfolio. Hub: Portfolio, Chat. Log: Portfolio, Research, Chat. Colophon: Portfolio, Lab log, Research.
6. **No LinkedIn link anywhere on the site.**
7. **Homepage hash landings vary by about 30px**; the section's top padding sometimes sits under the nav. Headings stay visible.

Could not verify (bot-blocked): epam.com, researchgate.net, data.worldbank.org, digitaltwinconsortium.org, dl.acm.org, direct.mit.edu.

## Phone (390×844, 320×640, 768×1024)

1. **Full résumé is squeezed to an 86px text column at 320px.** Stacked padding: `details` at x=46, `.resumeTimelineWrap` 15px, `.col-md-9` 15px, `.resumeList` 40px. The section is 16,040px tall; long words spill past the card.
2. **Field dialogue card opens off screen on phones.** Below 900px the card is `position: relative` after `.fieldWorld`; `select()` in `js/portfolio-field.mjs` never scrolls to it. At 320×640 nothing visible changes after a tap.
3. **Tapping "Reader" skips the card and jumps to Reading.** `js/script.js` document click handler calls `openHashTarget` for any `a[href^="#"]` without checking `e.defaultPrevented`. Introduced in the sell-homepage review fix.
4. **Reading carousel is cramped at 320.** A 160px cover in a 112px stage; blurb column about 100px.
5. **The round Ask button covers text**: proof-note line ends on the homepage, and body text on reports and essays at every scroll position.
6. **Tap targets under 44px:** menu links 35px; "Read the …" card links 26px; "More projects" links 23px; research filter buttons 33px; field cast buttons 36px; card close 32px.
7. **Text under 12px:** header links 11.52px; research eyebrow 10.72px; tags and meta 11.2px; résumé skill chips and certificate topics 10px.
8. **The menu stays open behind the Find dialog.** Not fixed: closing the menu hides the Find button, so focus cannot return to it when the dialog closes.
9. **Console error on homepage → research navigation:** "Transition was aborted because of invalid state". `css/research.css` opts in to view transitions and the homepage does not.

Passed clean: first screen (name, claim, buttons, first proof number with no scroll), hamburger links, Selected work reading at 320, reports at 320, research hub and essays, no sideways scroll at 320, 390 and 768.

## Desktop hiring manager (1440×900, 1280×800)

1. **Smoodee "Website" link is dead** (same as Links 1).
2. **Résumé dates overlap and are unreadable.** `css/style.css` `.resumeDateLoc { line-height: 0.5em }`; the date wraps to two lines that print on top of each other. Every work and education entry.
3. **Company logo overlaps the company name and role** at 1280; touching at 1440.
4. **`tony-koo.json` panel shows no punctuation.** Braces, colons and commas inherit the dark text colour on the `#1e1e1e` panel. Link values show stray spaces inside the quotes.
5. **Reader skips its dialogue card** (same as Phone 3).
6. **Field character labels overflow their pills** on first paint: "Researcher Research", "Student Background", "AI Engineer Projects". `.personaLabel { max-width: 118px; white-space: nowrap }`.
7. **One writing thumbnail is cropped mid-word** ("Designing Human-in-the-Loop AI Systems…").
8. **Projects lands about 150px lower than other sections**, with the bottom of the field art showing under the nav.
9. **No favicon on reports** (same as Links 4).
10. **Uneven card heights** in the certifications row and the testimonials row.

Confusing, not rendering bugs:
- The "Self-RL & RAG Extraction Pipelines" card opens a page titled "Using FAISS Caching to Reduce LLM Cost and Latency".
- The header reads "Case study" on reports labelled "Technical Note" or "Research-Style Paper".
- The same four reports appear twice on the homepage, in Selected work and in Technical Writing, under different titles.

Passed clean: first screen at both sizes; all four featured cards reach their reports and return; nav links; Find opens, searches and closes with focus returned; book carousel; 7 of 8 field characters; no broken images; no sideways scroll.

## Robustness (theme, keyboard, no JavaScript, reduced motion, zoom)

1. **Résumé dates overlap** (same as Desktop 2), in both themes.
2. **Day mode: JSON punctuation invisible** (same as Desktop 4). Night shows it.
3. **Night: writing-card links are 2.1:1.** `.writingLink` is `#315d4e` with no night override.
4. **No JavaScript at 390: the hamburger is dead**, so no nav link is reachable. Content is still reachable by scrolling.
5. **Night: `.accent-success` text is 3.05:1.**
6. **The homepage has no skip link**; the inner pages have one.
7. **Space does not open a field character.** Enter does. The element advertises `aria-expanded`.
8. **Day: the Ask launcher's focus ring is about 1.7:1.**
9. **Night: light boxes left on a dark page**: certification icon tiles; writing thumbnails and report figures keep white backgrounds.
10. **No favicon on inner pages** (same as Links 4).
11. **Tab order in the field toolbar is reversed**: cast buttons before the season buttons to their left.

Dead controls with JavaScript off: Find, the theme toggle (shows a bare "◐" on inner pages), season buttons, the hamburger at 390, book carousel buttons, research filter tabs.

Passed clean: night persists across every page with no light flash; toggle labels are correct; every keyboard stop has a visible ring; focus never lands in hidden content; Find and Ask open, trap focus, close on Escape and return focus; reduced motion (nothing animates); 200% zoom (no overlap or sideways scroll).
